IsoFoundry Portable

Open source disc burning, imaging and bootable USB suite. Packaged in PortableApps.com Format.

Start IsoFoundryPortable.exe. Settings and logs are kept in Data\settings beside it; nothing is written to the computer's AppData or registry.

Command line: run App\IsoFoundry\isofoundry.com --help.
